# Chrysi-Kalpakidou
Chrysi's data
PL-FC Process-Evaluation Dataset (De-identified), 2022–23 and 2023–24

De-identified response-level data from two consecutive implementations of the Peer-Learning Flipped Classroom (PL-FC) model in the first-year Mathematics course of the Department of Civil Engineering, Democritus University of Thrace.

This repository is the data deposit accompanying the doctoral thesis Flipped Learning and the Reshaping of Skills: A Case Study in Higher Education for the Development of Students' Soft Skills (University of Patras, Department of Business Administration). The instrument that generated the data was designed by G. S. Androulakis, originator of the PL-FC model, and is reproduced in full in Appendix A of the thesis.

Table B1 holds the 593 returns of 2022–23. Table B2 holds the 762 returns of 2023–24.

A row is one return, not one respondent. The questionnaire was completed once upon each presenting team's turn, so a student present at a session in which several teams presented contributed a return upon each of them. The within-student link is not preserved and cannot be reconstructed from these files. The two corpora are never pooled.

Columns

ID — sequential return code. P1–P593 for 2022–23, PR1–PR762 for 2023–24. The two prefixes keep the series distinct, so one code can never denote returns in two years at once.

Q3–Q9 — the closed rating items, each on a 0–4 scale. The verbal anchors differ between the two blocks:

Items 3–7, rating the peer-instructor team's teaching performance for the taught section: 0 is the lowest point, 4 is excellent.
Items 8–9, rating the collaborative climate among students during exercise-solving: 0 = καθόλου (not at all), 1 = λίγο (a little), 2 = αρκετά (moderately), 3 = πολύ (a lot), 4 = πάρα πολύ (very much).

Because the digit fully determines the anchor on either scale, the digit alone is recorded. No rating cell is empty in either year.

Item 10 — the verbatim free-response answer, in the respondent's original Greek, with spelling and punctuation as submitted. This is the open-ended item on perceived benefits and suggested changes, and it names no competence in advance.

One cell is blank in Table B1 (P247) and nine in Table B2 (PR384, PR408, PR457, PR480, PR508, PR535, PR567, PR604, PR645), each marking a return on which Item 10 went unanswered. Two cells in Table B2 (PR407, PR612) hold an ellipsis the respondent typed; a redaction is told apart from it by the square brackets.

De-identification

Items 1 and 2 of the questionnaire — full name and student registration number — were collected solely to verify that each return came from a student formally enrolled in and attending the course. They are not released in any form.

In the 2022–23 file the Item 1 answers were replaced by the sequential codes P1–P593 and the Item 2 answers deleted in their entirety. In the 2023–24 file both items had already been stripped from the response file before it reached the author, so nothing answering to either is held at all.

Where a respondent named a presenting group, or named an individual student, that identifier is replaced by […]. Three substitutions were made in 2022–23, at P77, P195 and P476, all of presenting groups. Six substitutions were made in 2023–24, across five returns: PR60, PR112, PR199, PR386 and PR564. No other alteration was made to any answer.

The files carry no names, no registration numbers, no e-mail addresses, no telephone numbers, no dates, no timestamps and no gender column. No entry in either table is traceable to a named individual, and no attempt to re-identify any participant is permitted.

The questionnaire items
Πόσο καλά λειτούργησαν οι εκπαιδευτές ως ομάδα κατά την διδασκαλία τους; (How well did the instructors function as a team during their teaching?)
Σε τι βαθμό κατάφερε η ομάδα των εκπαιδευτών να σας βοηθήσει να κατανοήσετε το περιεχόμενο της ενότητας που δίδαξε; (To what extent did the team of instructors manage to help you understand the content of the section they taught?)
Σε τι βαθμό κατάφεραν τα μέλη της ομάδας των εκπαιδευτών να λύσουν τις απορίες σας; (To what extent did the members of the team of instructors manage to resolve your questions?)
Χρησιμοποίησε η ομάδα των εκπαιδευτών επαρκείς τρόπους παρουσίασης και επεξήγησης της ύλης που δίδαξαν; (πίνακες, διαγράμματα, παραδείγματα, βίντεο, πηγές) (Did the team of instructors use adequate means of presenting and explaining the material they taught — tables, diagrams, examples, video, sources?)
Κατάφερε η ομάδα των εκπαιδευτών να σας κρατήσει ζωντανό το ενδιαφέρον κατά τη διάρκεια της εκπαιδευτικής διαδικασίας, βοηθώντας σας να συμμετέχετε πιο ενεργά ως ακροατήριο; (Did the team of instructors manage to keep your interest alive during the educational process, helping you to participate more actively as an audience?)
Πιστεύετε ότι η διδασκαλία από τους συμφοιτητές σας, σας βοήθησε κατά την επίλυση της άσκησης; (Do you believe that the teaching by your fellow students helped you during the solving of the exercise?)
Υπήρξε κλίμα συνεργασίας με τους συμφοιτητές σας κατά την επίλυση της άσκησης; (Was there a climate of cooperation with your fellow students during the solving of the exercise?)
Ποια θεωρείτε ότι είναι τα οφέλη που αποκομίσατε από την όλη διαδικασία και ποιές είναι οι αλλαγές που θα κάνατε; (What do you consider to be the benefits you gained from the whole process, and what changes would you make?)
